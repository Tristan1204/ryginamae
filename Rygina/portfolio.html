Rygina Mae Bolante

I Carried Their Love as My Fuel

From Siomai seller to scholar, from loss to purpose — this is my story of **Resilience and Faith**.

Hi, I’m Rygina Mae Bolante, born and raised in Rizal, Philippines. Life didn’t hand me an easy path — I grew up without my parents, but the unwavering love of my **Lola**, the kindness of others, and the grace of God fueled every step. Today, I’m a proud **BSBA Marketing Management graduate** and a scholar of Caritas Manila, Unbound, and Ynares Youth — living proof that dreams bloom even from hardship.
