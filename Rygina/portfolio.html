My Journey of Resilience and Faith

From Siomai seller to scholar, from loss to purpose — this is my story.

Hi, I'm Rygina Mae Bolante, born and raised in Rizal, Philippines. Life didn't hand me an easy path — I grew up without my parents, but I was never truly alone. Through the love of my grandmother, the kindness of others, and the grace of God, I found strength to keep going.

Today, I'm a proud BSBA Marketing Management graduate and a scholar of Caritas Manila, Unbound, and Ynares Youth — living proof that dreams bloom even from hardship.
